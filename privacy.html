HealthLab Privacy Policy

HealthLab is a personal application used only by its developer, for the developer's own Google Health data.

It requests read-only access to the Google Health data of the signed-in account. The data is stored on the developer's own private server, is used only to generate personal health reports, and is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. No other users' data is collected.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account permissions page, and the stored data deleted on request.
